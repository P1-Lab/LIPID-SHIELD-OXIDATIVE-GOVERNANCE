  <section style="margin-bottom: 25px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 18px; color: #24292e; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaecef; padding-bottom: 5px;">I. OBJECTIVE</h2>
    <p style="font-size: 14px;">
      This protocol defines the high-shear mechanical method for applying the <strong>Lipid-Shield™</strong> to dry particulate matrices (e.g., proteins, snack bases, or nut meats). The goal is to achieve 100% surface coverage with a uniform 5-micron lamination via kinetic energy rather than thermal binding.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 25px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 18px; color: #24292e; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaecef; padding-bottom: 5px;">II. EQUIPMENT CONFIGURATION</h2>
    <ul style="font-size: 14px; color: #24292e;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>Primary Mixer:</strong> High-shear plow mixer or ribbon blender with secondary intensifiers.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;"><strong>Impeller Speed:</strong> 1500–3000 RPM (Intensifier) / 30–60 RPM (Main Shaft).</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0;"><strong>Atomization (Optional):</strong> Pressure-nozzle delivery for concentrated lipid scaffolds.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 25px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 18px; color: #24292e; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaecef; padding-bottom: 5px;">III. KINETIC DEPLOYMENT SEQUENCE</h2>
    <ol style="font-size: 14px; color: #24292e; padding-left: 20px;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Substrate Loading:</strong> Charge the mixer with the base particulate. Ensure moisture content is &lt; 5% for optimal lamination.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>Shield Induction:</strong> Introduce <strong>LS-10-ALPHA</strong> concentrate at a rate of 0.1% to 0.2% by mass.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 10px;"><strong>High-Shear Activation:</strong> Engage intensifiers for 180 seconds. The kinetic energy facilitates the "Dry-Toss" lamination, driving the 5-micron particles into the surface texture of the substrate.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0;"><strong>Vitrification Hold:</strong> Allow the matrix to rest for 10 minutes post-blend to ensure the vitrified lipid scaffold stabilizes at the molecular interface.</li>
    </ol>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 25px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 18px; color: #24292e; border-bottom: 1px solid #eaecef; padding-bottom: 5px;">IV. QUALITY ASSURANCE (QA) CHECK</h2>
    <p style="font-size: 14px;">
      Verify lamination integrity via <strong>Surface Tension Audit</strong> (target: 38-45 dynes/cm). Failure to reach dyne targets indicates insufficient kinetic energy or high substrate moisture interference.
    </p>
  </section>
